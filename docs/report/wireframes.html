Summary5

This is a classic “digital arrest” scam. The caller impersonates police and CBI officers, claims your Aadhaar is linked to money laundering and demands a transfer to a fake “RBI verification” account. No real Indian agency arrests people over video calls.

Red flags found 56
Fake authority

“This is Inspector R. K. Sharma, Cyber Crime Branch, Mumbai Police.”

Impersonation of police and CBI to create instant fear.

Isolation / secrecy

“Do not tell anyone in your family or your bank.”

Stops the victim from verifying the claim with others.

Payment demand

“Transfer Rs 4,85,000 to the RBI verification account.”

Real agencies never ask citizens to move money to “prove innocence”.

tactic: PAYMENT_DEMAND · evidence = exact quote
